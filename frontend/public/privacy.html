Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

Billtiq ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By using Billtiq, you agree to the practices described here. If you don't agree, please don't use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account or accept a workspace invitation, we collect your name, email address, and the password you set (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, profile picture, and a unique Google identifier.

Workspace and Customer Data. When you use the Service, you and your workspace administrators submit data including invoice details, purchase order data, vendor information, line-item amounts, payment statuses, and related accounts payable information ("Customer Data"). We process this data on your behalf to provide the Service.

Usage data. We automatically collect information about how you interact with the Service, including pages visited, features used, IP address, browser type, device type, and timestamps. This helps us improve the product and detect abuse.

Communications. If you email us, book a demo, or message us through the Service, we keep records of those communications.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Process invoices, validate matches, and run workflows you configure
  • Communicate with you about your account, the Service, and product updates
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues and security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use Customer Data to train machine learning models without your explicit consent.

3. How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to provide the Service:

  • Within your workspace: Other members of your workspace can see Customer Data and account information you submit, according to your workspace's role and permission settings.
  • With service providers: We use trusted third parties to host the Service and provide essential infrastructure, including Supabase (database and authentication), Vercel (frontend hosting), Render (backend hosting), Resend (transactional email), and Stripe (payment processing). These providers process data on our behalf under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • With ERP integrations you authorize: When you connect an ERP system (e.g., Oracle Fusion, NetSuite, QuickBooks), data flows to and from that system based on the configurations you set.
  • For legal reasons: We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Billtiq, our users, or others.
  • In a business transfer: If Billtiq is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), encryption at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If we become aware of a security breach affecting your information, we will notify you and applicable authorities as required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain Customer Data for as long as your workspace is active. If your workspace is deleted or you terminate your account, we delete or anonymize Customer Data within a reasonable period (typically 30 days), except where we're required to retain it for legal, accounting, or audit purposes.

Account information and usage logs may be retained longer for legitimate business and security purposes.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Portability: Request export of your information in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain types of processing

To exercise these rights, email us at contact@billtiq.com. We will respond within a reasonable period and within timeframes required by applicable law.

7. California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the rights described above. We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

8. International Users

Billtiq is operated from the United States. If you access the Service from outside the US, your information will be transferred to and processed in the US, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

9. Cookies and Tracking

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, but parts of the Service may not function correctly without them.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or through the Service. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects when this policy was last changed.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at contact@billtiq.com.
